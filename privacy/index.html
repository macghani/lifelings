Lifelings

Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Lifelings is a life game for iPhone. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

What Lifelings collects

Nothing. Lifelings has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The app does not send any information about you or your play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your game, including the person you are raising, their name, looks, home, wardrobe, family and diary, is saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

Settings can copy a backup code to your clipboard. It contains your saved game and nothing else. Where you paste it is up to you; we never see it.

If you match your character to a photo, the app reads colours from the photo you pick or take. The photo is used on the phone only, is not saved by the app and never leaves your device. You can skip this feature entirely.

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules at most two local notifications a day on your phone. No notification server is involved, and you can turn them off in Settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera and Photos: only when you tap Match a photo, and only to pick colours for your character.
  • Notifications: only when you turn on reminders.

Each can be refused, and the game works without them.

Children

Lifelings is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no information, it does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact
