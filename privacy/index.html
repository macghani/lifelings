Lifelings

Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Lifelings is a life game for iPhone and iPad. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your game stays on your device and in your own iCloud, and we never see any of it.

What Lifelings collects

Nothing. Lifelings has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The app does not send any information about you or your play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device and in your iCloud

Your game, including the person you are raising, their name, looks, home, wardrobe, family and diary, is saved on your device, in the app's own storage.

If you use iCloud, your game is also saved in your own iCloud account, so a new iPhone or iPad on the same Apple Account brings it back. We can't see what's in your iCloud: it goes from your device to Apple and back, never to us. Deleting the app on one device doesn't delete the copy in iCloud. If you aren't signed in to iCloud, the game is saved on this device only.

The save holds the game and nothing else: the names you chose, the story so far, coins and the list of coin packs bought (so a pack is never counted twice). Photos never go into the save, on the device or in iCloud. Apple stores it encrypted, like the rest of your iCloud data, and nothing in it is more personal than the names you picked.

Settings can copy a backup code to your clipboard. It contains your saved game and nothing else. Where you paste it is up to you; we never see it.

If you match your character to a photo, the app reads colours from the photo you pick or take. The photo is used on the phone only, is not saved by the app and never leaves your device. Only the colours are kept, as part of how your character looks. You can skip this feature entirely.

If you make a picture card (a birthday party photo, a "Day" card or a life story), it is made on your device from a picture of the game's own room. Sharing it uses your device's share sheet, so you choose where it goes; Lifelings never posts anything and nothing is sent to us. Saving it to Photos only adds the picture: Lifelings can't see your other photos.

If you add the Lifelings widget to your home screen, it shows a short summary that the app leaves on your device: your person's name and age and what they're up to. It stays on the device.

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules at most three local notifications a day on your device. No notification server is involved, and you can turn them off in Settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera and Photos: only when you tap Match a photo (My photo when you play as yourself), and only to pick colours for your character.
  • Adding to Photos: only when you tap Save to Photos on a picture card. It lets Lifelings add a picture, not see your photos.
  • Notifications: only when you turn on reminders.

Each can be refused, and the game works without them.

Children

People of all ages play Lifelings, children included. It collects nothing from anyone, whatever their age, so there is nothing about a child to collect, keep or share. On an Apple Account shared by a family, everyone using that account can see the families saved in its iCloud, and Lifelings says so when it asks which family is yours.
