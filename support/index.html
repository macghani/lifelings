Support

Lifelings is a life game for iPhone and iPad: a person is born, and you raise them (or live the life yourself) one real day at a ti

Why is nothing happening?

At the normal pace, one real day is one in-game season, so a whole life takes about a year, and each care button waits a couple of hours after you use it. In between you can give a cuddle, play Bubble pop or go out somewhere. If you'd rather watch a life unfold quickly, start a new life and choose Speedy (an hour is a season) or Whirlwind (a minute is a season).

I was away and my person is in hospital.

Needs keep draining while the app is closed, but they are capped so a weekend away is survivable. A hospital stay is recovery time, not the end. You can visit the doctor to speed it up, or rest at home. The doctor is free for children and for anyone 65 or over; grown-ups of working age pay 60 coins.

Looking after a big family is a lot.

In the Family tab, turn on Auto for anyone. Mum and Dad look after brothers and sisters for free, and a childminder looks after your own children (a kind neighbour, for free, once you've retired). They'll be fed, washed and rested, though looking after them yourself keeps them happiest.

Can I move my game to a new phone?

Yes. From version 1.3, if iCloud is on, sign in to the new iPhone or iPad with the same Apple Account and open Lifelings. It looks for your game for a few seconds, then asks "Is this your game?". Tap "Yes, that's mine" and your family is back, coins and all.

Playing on an iPhone and an iPad at the same time works too: each one catches up with the other when you open it. If a catch-up ever takes you to the wrong copy, Settings has "Go back to an earlier save", which swaps the two and keeps both.

To move a game to someone else's Apple Account, use a backup code. In Settings, tap Copy backup code and paste the code somewhere safe, such as Notes. On the other device, paste it into the same box and tap Restore.

My game didn't come back on my new iPad.

Give it a minute with Wi-Fi on, then close Lifelings and open it again. If you've already started a new family, that's fine: when your old family turns up, Lifelings asks if you want to go back to it, and the new family stays saved too.

If it still doesn't come back, check that iCloud is on for this iPad in the Settings app (tap your name at the top, then iCloud) and that it's the same Apple Account as before. A backup code always works too.

Two children share one Apple Account.

Each family is saved on its own, so one child's game never overwrites the other's. On a new iPad, Lifelings asks which family is yours and shows when each was last played, so pick your own. Everyone using that Apple Account can see the families saved in it.

If you'd rather keep them apart completely, Apple recommends each person has their own Apple Account; Family Sharing lets you share purchases between them.

Lifelings says the game is only saved on this iPad.

That means this iPad isn't signed in to iCloud, so the game is kept on this iPad only. It plays exactly the same. A grown-up can sign in to iCloud in the Settings app, and the game is saved there from then on. Until then, a backup code from Settings is a good way to keep it safe.

I started a new life by mistake.

"Start a new life" asks twice, so this is rare. When it does happen, the old family isn't deleted from iCloud, but this device won't offer it again. If it was also being played on another iPhone or iPad, it carries on there as before. To bring it back to this device, restore a backup code of it, if you copied one.

I tapped "Start a new family" on a new iPad, but it was my game.

Nothing is lost. Your game is still saved in iCloud. If you haven't started a new family yet, tap "Find my game in iCloud" under the welcome words, then "Yes, that's mine". If you already started one, play your game on your old iPhone or iPad within three days, and Lifelings will ask about it again on the new one.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Lifelings needs the internet only to download and update it, and to keep your game up to date in iCloud. If you're offline, the game is saved on the device and catches up with iCloud next time you're online. It never connects to a server of ours.

My brother or sister moved out.

Everyone moves into a place of their own at 18. Visit them from the Family tab or Go out.

Where do coins come from?

Coins are the only money in the game. They come from jobs, pocket money, the pension, daily goals, birthdays, Bubble pop, the first club session each season, catching a big moment, life cards and the occasional surprise gift for reaching a skill milestone. They pay for everything: rent, meals, trips out, furniture, homes, clubs, the doctor and the wardrobe. Tap the coin count at the top of the screen to see where your coins came from and where they went.

How do pets work?

Open the Family tab to bring home a rabbit, cat, dog, puffling or baby dragon, up to three at once, and give them a name. Feed them and play with them now and then. Happy pets cheer up the whole house each season. A pet that's been forgotten gets hungry or bored, but it never dies or runs away. Mum and Dad pay for pet food until your person turns 18.

Can my child buy coins by mistake?

Tapping a coin pack first asks a grown-up question (a sum written in words, like "seventeen times six"; two wrong answers within five minutes rest it for a minute). A right answer counts for 15 minutes, and if a grown-up has turned buying on in Settings the game doesn't ask again. Then Apple asks for Face ID or the account password as usual. To stop buying altogether, open Settings (the ... button) → Buying coins and turn it off; turning it back on needs the grown-up answer. Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time limits work too. In the EU and EEA, buying starts off until a grown-up turns it on.

How do I share a birthday party photo?

Right after you tap Collect on a birthday, a little "Party photo?" card shows over the room for a minute. Tap "Take one" and Lifelings makes a picture of the party with the person's name, their new age, the date and which day of their life it is. Then tap "Share" to send it with your iPhone's own share sheet, or "Save to Photos" to keep it.

You can also make a "Day 37 card" of today from the Life tab, and at the end of a life, "Share Ada's life story" puts the whole life on one picture.

Before a picture leaves the game, Lifelings asks the grown-up question (a sum written in words). Lifelings never posts anything itself: the share sheet lets you choose where the picture goes, or nowhere at all. Saving to Photos only asks to add pictures, so Lifelings can't see any of your photos. If you said no to that by mistake, open the iPhone Settings app, then Lifelings, then Photos. Pictures can be shared from the iPhone and iPad app, not the web version.

How do I turn reminders off?

Settings in the app, or the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.

Something's wrong

Open an issue at github.com/macghani/lifelings/issues with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Please don't include your backup code in a public issue.
