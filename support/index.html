Lifelings

Support

Lifelings is a life game for iPhone: a person is born, and you raise them (or live the life yourself) one real day at a time.

Why is nothing happening?

At the normal pace, one real day is one in-game season, so a whole life takes about a year, and each care button waits a couple of hours after you use it. In between you can give a cuddle, play Bubble pop or go out somewhere. If you'd rather watch a life unfold quickly, start a new life and choose Speedy (an hour is a season) or Whirlwind (a minute is a season).

I was away and my person is in hospital.

Needs keep draining while the app is closed, but they are capped so a weekend away is survivable. A hospital stay is recovery time, not the end. You can pay the doctor with coins to speed it up, or rest at home.

Looking after a big family is a lot.

In the Family tab, turn on Auto for anyone. Mum and Dad look after brothers and sisters for free, and a childminder looks after your own children. They'll be fed, washed and rested, though looking after them yourself keeps them happiest.

Can I move my game to a new phone?

Yes. In Settings, tap Copy backup code and paste the code somewhere safe, such as Notes. On the new phone, paste it into the same box and tap Restore.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Lifelings needs the internet only to download and update it. The game, its save and its reminders all stay on your phone, and it never connects to a server.

My brother or sister moved out.

Everyone moves into a place of their own at 18. Visit them from the Family tab or Go out.

Where do coins come from?

Coins are the only money in the game. They come from jobs, pocket money, the pension, daily goals, birthdays, Bubble pop, the first club session each season, catching a big moment, life cards and the occasional surprise gift for reaching a skill milestone. They pay for everything: rent, meals, trips out, furniture, homes, clubs, the doctor and the wardrobe. Tap the coin count at the top of the screen to see where your coins came from and where they went.

How do pets work?

From version 1.2, open the Family tab to bring home a rabbit, cat, dog, puffling or baby dragon, up to three at once, and give them a name. Feed them and play with them now and then. Happy pets cheer up the whole house each season. A pet that's been forgotten gets hungry or bored, but it never dies or runs away. Mum and Dad pay for pet food until your person turns 18.

Can my child buy coins by mistake?

Tapping a coin pack first asks a grown-up question (a times-table sum), and then Apple asks for Face ID or the account password as usual. To stop buying altogether, open Settings (the ... button) → Buying coins and turn it off; turning it back on needs the grown-up answer. Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time limits work too. In the EU, buying starts off until a grown-up turns it on.

What happened to dollars?

Version 1.1 joined dollars and coins into one currency. Any dollars in your save were added to your coins, one for one, so nothing was lost.

How do I turn reminders off?

Settings in the app, or the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.

Something's wrong

Open an issue at github.com/macghani/lifelings/issues with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Please don't include your backup code in a public issue.
