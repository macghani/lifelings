Lifelings

Support

Lifelings is a life game for iPhone: a person is born, and you raise them (or live the life yourself) one real day at a time.

Why is nothing happening?

At the normal pace, one real day is one in-game season, so a whole life takes about a year. Check in once or twice a day to feed, wash and play, and take them out somewhere. If you'd rather watch a life unfold quickly, start a new life and choose Two weeks or An afternoon.

I was away and my person is in hospital.

Needs keep draining while the app is closed, but they are capped so a weekend away is survivable. A hospital stay is recovery time, not the end. You can pay the doctor with coins to speed it up, or rest at home.

Looking after a big family is a lot.

In the Family tab, turn on Auto for anyone. Mum and Dad look after brothers and sisters for free; your partner or a childminder looks after your own children. They'll be fed, washed and rested, though looking after them yourself keeps them happiest.

Can I move my game to a new phone?

Yes. In Settings, tap Copy backup code and paste the code somewhere safe, such as Notes. On the new phone, paste it into the same box and tap Restore.

Where do coins come from?

Daily goals, birthdays and seasonal events. Coins buy outfits in the Wardrobe, and outfits can be traded back in for coins.

How do I turn reminders off?

Settings in the app, or the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.

Something's wrong

Open an issue at github.com/macghani/lifelings/issues with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Please don't include your backup code in a public issue.
