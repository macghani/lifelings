Raise a life, a day at a time.

A baby is born on your phone. Give them a name and a look, then look after them: bottles, naps, baths, cuddles and play. One real day is one season of their life, so a whole life takes about a year.

They grow up, go to school, join clubs, get a job and raise a family of their own. Take them for a pram walk, and later to the playground, soft play or the arcade, dressed for the season. When their life ends, carry on as one of their children.

No account, no adverts and no tracking. Your game stays on your phone.
